# Positive Autism design brief

Design read: families, educators and autistic adults looking for calm, trustworthy, hopeful guidance. Warm, gentle, never clinical.

Concept spine: a paper garden. Everything is handmade paper in the logo's teal and orange; the heart blossom opens as the visitor reads.

Delivery tier: editorial (calm audience, sensory-friendly). Micro-motion only, fully reduced-motion safe.

Animation mode: non-animated - user picked Non-animated at intake after the film could not be generated (workspace out of video credits).

Locked palette (brand colours from the logo): teal 167A83 (text/accent), logo teal 46BCC4, logo orange E9A15A, mint paper F2FAF8, deep ink 12333A, sky CDEAF5, gold F6C453. Defense: taken straight from the logo and the generated paper-craft art.

Locked type: Outfit (display and body, already loaded by the root), high contrast, generous line height.

Section plan (families): 1 hero split, 2 two audience panels, 3 ruled resource list, 4 video feature, 5 news row, 6 growth strip of 6 paper stages, 7 community band, 8 footer.

Asset plan: hero + together paper-craft scenes, 6 bloom stages, logo, favicon, OG/cover, article thumbnails from the source site.

CTA inventory: Explore resources (hero, panels), Read the news, Watch the film, Contact us. Each its own component class.
